Five-Month AWS SDE Study Roadmap

This roadmap turns the AWS SDE job requirements into a daily study system: coding fundamentals, distributed systems, object-oriented design, and Leadership Principles practice.

Duration 20 weeks
Daily Time 2.5 to 4 hours
Target 180 to 220 LeetCode problems

Daily Routine

How Each Study Day Works

Keep the routine consistent. The point is not just to solve problems, but to explain the pattern, complexity, edge cases, and trade-offs clearly.

30m Concept review: DSA, OS, networking, database, or design
90-120m Two LeetCode problems with written complexity analysis
45-60m System design, object-oriented design, or review
20m Error log, flash review, or STAR behavioral notes

Monthly Track

What to Study Each Month

The plan starts with interview coding patterns and gradually shifts into system design, object-oriented design, and mock interview mode.

Month 1

DSA Foundation

Arrays, HashMap, Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Stack, Queue, Binary Search, Linked List, and recursion basics.

  • Core problems: Two Sum, Contains Duplicate, Group Anagrams
  • Core problems: 3Sum, Minimum Window Substring, LRU Cache
  • Goal: explain why each pattern fits the problem
Month 2

Trees, Graphs, and Heap

Build comfort with recursion, BFS, DFS, priority queues, graph traversal, and topological sort.

  • Core problems: Validate BST, Binary Tree Level Order
  • Core problems: Number of Islands, Course Schedule
  • Goal: choose BFS, DFS, heap, or graph modeling quickly
Month 3

DP, Backtracking, Intervals, and Greedy

Practice state definition, recurrence relations, decision trees, pruning, interval sorting, and greedy proofs.

  • Core problems: Subsets, Combination Sum, Word Search
  • Core problems: Coin Change, Word Break, Merge Intervals
  • Goal: write the state and transition before coding DP
Month 4

System Design and Object-Oriented Design

Move from coding patterns into scalable service design, distributed systems, operations, and object modeling.

  • Design: URL Shortener, Rate Limiter, Notification System
  • Design: Key-value Store, Logging System, Chat System
  • OOD: Parking Lot, Elevator, Library, Vending Machine
Month 5

Interview Simulation

Shift from learning new concepts to timed execution, explanation, review, and behavioral storytelling.

  • Amazon tagged LeetCode problems by topic
  • Full mocks: coding, design, and behavioral rounds
  • Goal: answer clearly under time pressure

System Design

Design Answer Framework

For AWS-style interviews, mention reliability, scaling, monitoring, and operational trade-offs. Do not stop at naming a component.

Answer Order

  1. Clarify functional and non-functional requirements
  2. Estimate scale: QPS, storage, bandwidth, and growth
  3. Define APIs and request/response shapes
  4. Choose data models and storage systems
  5. Draw the high-level architecture
  6. Deep dive into cache, queues, partitions, and consistency
  7. Explain failure handling, retries, timeouts, and fallback
  8. Define metrics, logging, alerts, and dashboards

Must-Know Topics

Load Balancing Cache CDN SQL vs NoSQL Sharding Replication Queues Idempotency Rate Limiting Monitoring

Behavioral

AWS Leadership Principles Practice

Prepare 10 to 12 STAR stories. Each story should include your specific actions, measurable results, and what you learned.

STAR Structure

Situation: context. Task: your responsibility. Action: what you personally did. Result: measurable outcome, impact, or lesson.

Stories to Prepare

Customer impact, ambiguity, failure recovery, disagreement, root-cause investigation, fast execution, long-term improvement, and mentoring or team growth.

Principles to Connect

Customer Obsession, Ownership, Learn and Be Curious, Bias for Action, Dive Deep, Earn Trust, Have Backbone, and Deliver Results.

Final Readiness Check

You can solve Medium problems in 35 to 45 minutes, explain system design trade-offs for 45 minutes, and deliver STAR answers in roughly two minutes each.
